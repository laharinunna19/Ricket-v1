How To Play 🏏


            


                

                    1️⃣ Choose
                    

                        Select your programming language,
                        IPL team and difficulty.
                    

                


                

                    2️⃣ Code
                    

                        Solve the coding challenge before
                        the timer runs out.
                    

                


                

                    3️⃣ Score
                    

                        90–100% → 6 runs

                        70–89% → 4 runs

                        40–69% → 2 runs

                        Below 40% → Dot ball
                    

                


                

                    4️⃣ Wicket
                    

                        If the timer expires, you lose
                        your wicket.
                    

                


                

                    5️⃣ Match
                    

                        Six balls make one over.
                        Complete three overs to finish
                        the match.
